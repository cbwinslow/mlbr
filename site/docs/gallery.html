Q-CRISP Study 15 Lineups & K-Props

89,118 exact starting lineup slots (2024–2025) & 9,714 MLB starts: Poisson Strikeout Prop engine combining SP prior K%, opposing 9-man lineup K%, arm-slot ride, park pf_so, umpire K-factor & catcher framing delivers locked-2025 Over 5.5 K AUC 0.6638 (+0.0228 lift).

89,118 Lineup Slots | 4,859 OOS Starts | Over 5.5 K AUC 0.6638 | Interactive Plotly

F5 AUC 0.5846 | Totals -0.0118 R/G | 50k Exact 12-Team Brackets | Interactive Plotly

Q-CRISP Study 06 Level 3 Foundation

Empirical Ballpark Factors & Altitude Geometry

3-year rolling regressed park factors across 24 seasons (2002–2025) for all 30 MLB venues from 62,598 Retrosheet games and 16.2M+ play events. Quantifies altitude elevation lift (Coors Field ~122), small-sample shrinkage (M = 40 games), and handedness home run wall asymmetry.

Dataset: 16.2M Retrosheet Plays | M = 40 Games / M = 1500 PA | Interactive Plotly
Q-CRISP Study 05 Markov + Decisions

Q-CRISP Study 03 XGBoost + Tunneling

Unsupervised Pitch Clustering

K-Means and Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM) applied to 3D trajectory and break vectors (pfx_x, pfx_z, velocity, spin). Repertoire discovery without human pitch-tag bias.

Algorithm: K-Means / GMM | Dataset: Statcast Flight Dynamics
Supervised ML tidymodels

Pitch Type Classification with Random Forest

Multi-class classification using ranger and tidymodels to classify 6 distinct pitch types from raw flight physics. Out-of-fold ROC-AUC evaluation and confusion matrix diagnostics.

Algorithm: Random Forest (ranger) | Metrics: Accuracy & ROC-AUC

4-Quadrant Platoon Physics & PVI

Full-population RvR, RvL, LvL, and LvR analysis across 14.64M pitches: batter-perspective horizontal break (hb_away_in), CSW%, xwOBA, TTO decay, and Pitcher Platoon Vulnerability Index.

Sabermetrics GAM / LOESS

Non-Linear xwOBA & Barrel Landscape

Fitting a 2D Generalized Additive Model (GAM) thin-plate spline over Exit Velocity and Launch Angle to map the continuous probability landscape of hits, extra bases, and Statcast "Barrels".

Model: 2D Thin-Plate Spline (mgcv) | Batted Balls: Statcast
Sabermetrics Retrosheet

Run Expectancy RE24 Matrix

Empirical calculation of the 24 base-out run expectancy matrix directly from 23.8 million Retrosheet play events. Inning state transitions, run values per event, and game leverage indexing.

Dataset: 23.8M Retrosheet Plays | Coverage: 1871–2025
