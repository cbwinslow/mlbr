The mlbr Experiment Registry & Quantitative Knowledge Base

Curated Repository of Verified Models, Mathematical Formulas, Invariants & Negative Results

Author

mlbr Quantitative Research Group

Published

November 16, 2026

NoteResearch Philosophy: Curated Signal, Zero Clutter

This registry is machine-readable and human-navigable. It records verified mathematical invariants, empirical effect sizes, and crucially, rejected hypotheses. Recording what failed prevents future research cycles and autonomous AI agents from repeating dead ends.

1. Predictive Architecture Tiers

Every experiment maps directly to one of the five tiers in the mlbr hierarchical forecasting stack:

Tier Name Target Outcome Primary Models
Level 1 Pitch Physics & Quality \(\Pr(\text{Whiff} \mid \text{Swing})\), Run Value / 100 XGBoost, Random Forest, GAM
Level 2 Player Projections True Talent Rates (wOBA, K%, BB%) PECOTA Comps, ZiPS Aging Curves, Marcel
Level 3 Team BaseRuns Expected Runs Scored & Allowed BaseRuns Equation, Pythagenpat Exponents
Level 4 Game Simulation Win Probability \(\Pr(\text{Home Win})\) Monte Carlo Markov Chain (10k rollouts)
Level 5 Betting Markets Market Edge & Expected Value Fractional Kelly Staking (Polymarket / Kalshi)

2. Status Definitions

  • VALIDATED: Tested on historical data (\(\le 2023\)) and confirmed out-of-time (\(2024–2026\)). Active in production feature views.
  • IN_PROGRESS: In active exploratory data analysis or hyperparameter tuning.
  • REJECTED: Hypothesis tested and proven non-predictive or redundant (signals dead ends).
  • DEPRECATED: Previously validated model superseded by a superior architecture.

EXP-001-AERODYNAMICS: Trajectory Aerodynamics, Approach Angles (VAA/HAA) & Whiff Lift

Tier: Level 1: Pitch Physics & Quality | Status: VALIDATED | Updated: 2026-11-16

Derived full-population terminal flight velocities at y=1.417 ft, VAA, HAA, plate_x_norm, and attack zones over 14.64M pitches in DuckDB. Proved flat VAA (VAA_diff > +1.5 deg) produces +52% relative lift in Four-Seam fastball whiff rate.

Verified Formulas

terminal_forward_velo: vy_f = -sqrt(vy0^2 + 2 * ay * (1.417 - 50.0))
flight_time: t_flight = (vy_f - vy0) / ay
vertical_approach_angle: vaa = atan2(vz0 + az * t_flight, -vy_f) * (180.0 / PI())
horizontal_approach_angle: haa = atan2(vx0 + ax * t_flight, -vy_f) * (180.0 / PI())
expected_four_seam_vaa: expected_vaa = -7.71 + 1.06 * plate_z
vaa_diff: vaa_diff = vaa - expected_vaa
plate_x_norm: plate_x * (case when stand = 'L' then -1.0 else 1.0 end)

EXP-002-PLATOON-PHYSICS: 4-Quadrant Platoon Physics, Break Symmetry & Situational Pitching Effectiveness

Tier: Level 1: Pitch Physics & Quality | Status: VALIDATED | Updated: 2026-11-18

Quantified full-population pitching mechanics across RvR, RvL, LvL, and LvR using Silver v_statcast_features. Established Batter-Perspective Horizontal Break (hb_away_in) as the physical driver of platoon splits: glove-side breaking pitches (ST/SL) lose 4.9-7.5 pct whiff and ~2-3 pct CSW against opposite-hand batters while offspeed (CH/FS) inverts the edge. Derived Pitcher Platoon Vulnerability Index (PVI) and verified out-of-time stability (r = 0.885).

Verified Formulas

hb_glove_in: hb_in * (CASE WHEN p_throws = 'L' THEN -1 ELSE 1 END)
hb_away_in: hb_in * (CASE WHEN stand = 'L' THEN -1 ELSE 1 END)
pvi_xwoba: xwOBA_opposite - xwOBA_same
pvi_csw: CSW_same - CSW_opposite
rv_per_100: AVG(COALESCE(delta_pitcher_run_exp, -delta_run_exp)) * 100

EXP-003-TUNNELING-STUFF-PLUS: Commit-Point Pitch Tunneling & Platoon-Conditioned Stuff+ / Location+ / Pitching+

Tier: Level 1: Pitch Physics & Quality | Status: VALIDATED | Updated: 2026-11-19

Materialized exact y0=50ft -> y=23.8ft commit-point pitch tunneling across all 19 seasons (10,201,496 consecutive pairs, strictly sorted ADR-001 ZSTD Parquet, 0 bad rows, 0 inversions) and trained strictly chronological Regular-Season (game_type=‘R’) xgboost hist Stuff+, orthogonal Location+, Pitching+, and Pitching+Tunnel models over 2015-2026. All tree models beat empirical and analytical Ridge baselines on locked 2025-2026 run value and swing-and-miss whiff deception; mlbr Stuff+ hits split-half r = 0.975 in 100 regular-season pitches (vs. 0.078 for Raw RV/100), achieves YoY r = 0.878 (vs. ERA r = 0.188), and correlates at r = 0.817 with FanGraphs Pitching+.

Verified Formulas

commit_time_t_dec: (-sqrt(vy0^2 + 2*ay*(23.8 - 50.0)) - vy0) / ay
x50_reconstruction: plate_x - vx0*t_plate - 0.5*ax*t_plate^2
tunnel_dist_in: 12 * sqrt((x_dec_N - x_dec_{N-1})^2 + (z_dec_N - z_dec_{N-1})^2)
break_to_tunnel_ratio_bp: post_tunnel_break_in / GREATEST(tunnel_dist_in, 1.0)
plate_to_tunnel_ratio: plate_sep_in / GREATEST(tunnel_dist_in, 1.0)
plus_scale_2024_lock: 100 + 10 * (pred_i - mu_2024_reg) / sd_pitcher_2024_reg_min250

EXP-005-DECOMPOSED-PITCHING-DECISIONS: Context-Neutral Markov Decomposed Pitching+ & Pitch-Level Batter Process Engine (Decision+, BatControl+, Contact+)

Tier: Level 1: Pitch Physics & Quality | Status: VALIDATED | Updated: 2026-11-20

Replaced single-head raw delta_run_exp regression with a strictly chronological 5-head Markov probability decomposition (p_swing, p_cstr|take, p_whiff|swing, p_foul|contact, E[xwOBA|BIP]) priced via 2015-2023 context-neutral 12-count linear weights. Decomp Pitching+ beat Study 03 Pitching+ on locked 2024->2025 SIERA (-0.5040 vs -0.4984), Hawk-Eye 2020-2023 beat 2015-2023 on all 5 pitch-level validation heads, and pitch-level Batter Decision+ achieved r=0.8464 YoY stability and r=+0.5583 forecasting 2025 BB% (crushing Z-O% proxy +0.3994).

Verified Formulas

bip_rv_map: delta_run_exp ~ factor(count_id) + 0.8593 * xwoba (fit strictly on 2015-2023 BIPs)
ev_take: p_cs * rv_strike(b,s) + (1 - p_cs) * rv_ball(b,s)
ev_swing: p_wh * rv_strike(b,s) + (1 - p_wh) * (p_fl * rv_foul(b,s) + (1 - p_fl) * rv_bip(b,s,xwoba_hat))
decision_voe: (is_swing - p_sw_hat) * (ev_swing - ev_take)
bat_ctrl_rv: is_swing * (p_wh_hat - is_whiff) * (ev_contact - rv_strike(b,s))
contact_rv: is_bip * 0.8593 * (xwoba - xwoba_hat)
batter_process_rv: decision_voe + bat_ctrl_rv + contact_rv

EXP-004-PLAYER-PROJECTIONS: Chronological True-Talent Player Projections: Marcel, Delta-Method Aging & Tier 1 Physics Priors

Tier: Level 2: Player Projections | Status: VALIDATED | Updated: 2026-11-20

Built audit-hardened Tier 2 chronological true-talent projection engine for MLB pitchers and batters across 2002-2027 (10-year rolling Tango Marcel vs. Midpoint Delta-Method Aging 2016-2025, 7-year Statcast Physics 2019-2025, locked 2024->2025 FanGraphs OOS with Stage-2 Residual mlbr Stuff+ and Hawk-Eye Bat Tracking, and secondary 2025->2026 Regular-Season Statcast OOS). Introduced Pulled Air-Ball%, Zone-Chase Selectivity (Z-O Swing%, SEAGER-inspired proxy), Sweet-Spot%, Fastball Velocity Momentum, sum-before-shrink seasonal PVI, and exposure-calibrated 80% uncertainty bands.

Verified Formulas

exact_ip_true: FLOOR(IP) + ROUND((IP - FLOOR(IP)) * 10) / 3
baseball_age_july1: Season - birth_year - I(birth_month >= 7)
tango_marcel_age: age <= 29: 1 + 0.006*(29 - age); age > 29: 1 / (1 + 0.003*(age - 29)) + Step 6 rebaselining
marcel_rate_shrinkage: mu_lg_{T-1,g} + SUM(w_k * N_{T-k} * (r_{T-k} - mu_lg_{T-k,g})) / (SUM(w_k * N_{T-k}) + M_r)
delta_aging_midpoint: lm(delta ~ ns(age - 0.5, knots = c(24.5, 30.5)), weights = 2*N_{s-1}*N_s/(N_{s-1}+N_s)) + linear boundary extrapolation
pulled_air_pct: mean(LA >= 10 & ((stand=='R' & spray < -15 deg) | (stand=='L' & spray > +15 deg)))
zone_chase_selectivity: Z-Swing% - O-Swing% (SEAGER-inspired box-zone proxy)
uncertainty_se: sqrt(sigma_skill^2 + sigma_noise^2 / exposure)

4. How to Register an Experiment

To log an experiment from an R script or console:

library(mlbr)

mlbr::lake_log_experiment(
  id         = "EXP-002-FASTBALL-DELTAS",
  title      = "Fastball-Relative Velocity Delta (ΔV) and Offspeed Deception",
  tier       = 1,
  status     = "IN_PROGRESS",
  summary    = "Evaluates offspeed swing-and-miss lift conditioned on velocity separation from primary fastball.",
  hypotheses = c("Changeups with ΔV > 10 mph generate +25% higher whiff rates than changeups with ΔV < 6 mph."),
  features   = c("delta_v", "delta_ivb", "delta_hb")
)

The function automatically updates: 1. experiments/registry.json (Machine-readable database) 2. experiments/README.md (Human-readable table) 3. data/manifest/agent_context.json (AI agent context)
